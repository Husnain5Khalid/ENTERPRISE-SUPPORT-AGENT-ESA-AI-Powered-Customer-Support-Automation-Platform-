# Internet Connectivity Troubleshooting

## Purpose
Provide a standard troubleshooting workflow for customers experiencing internet connectivity issues.

## Scope
Applies to all home broadband customers.

## Business Rules
- Follow troubleshooting steps in order.
- Do not schedule field visits before completing remote diagnostics.

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. Verify service status for the customer's area.
2. Confirm modem power is ON.
3. Restart the modem.
4. Wait two minutes.
5. Verify cable connections.
6. Run a remote line diagnostic.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate if:
- Remote diagnostics fail.
- A hardware fault is detected.
- The issue persists after all troubleshooting steps.
