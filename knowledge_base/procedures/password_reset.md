# Password Reset Procedure

## Purpose
This document defines the standard process for assisting customers who are unable to access their account due to a forgotten password.

## Scope
Applicable to all registered customers using the web portal or mobile application.

## Business Rules
- Password resets require identity verification.
- OTP is valid for 5 minutes.
- Maximum of 3 OTP attempts are allowed.
- Password must contain at least:
  - 8 characters
  - One uppercase letter
  - One lowercase letter
  - One number
  - One special character

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. Verify the customer's registered email or mobile number.
2. Send a One-Time Password (OTP).
3. Validate the OTP.
4. Allow the customer to create a new password.
5. Confirm the password reset was successful.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate the ticket if:
- OTP delivery fails after three attempts.
- The registered email or mobile number cannot be verified.
- The customer reports account compromise.
