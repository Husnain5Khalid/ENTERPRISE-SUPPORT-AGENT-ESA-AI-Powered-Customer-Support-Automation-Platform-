# SIM Card Activation Procedure

## Purpose
Guide customers through activating a new SIM card.

## Scope
Applies to prepaid and postpaid mobile customers.

## Business Rules
- Identity verification is mandatory.
- SIM activation is available only for registered customers.

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. Verify customer identity.
2. Validate the SIM serial number.
3. Activate the SIM in the provisioning system.
4. Inform the customer that activation may take up to 15 minutes.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate if:
- SIM serial number is invalid.
- Activation fails.
- Fraud is suspected.
