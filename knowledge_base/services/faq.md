# Purpose

Provide quick answers to common customer questions.

---

# Frequently Asked Questions

## What are your support hours?

Support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

---

## How can I contact support?

Customers can contact support through:

- Mobile application
- Website
- Email
- Customer hotline

---

## How do I update my profile?

Profile information can be updated from the customer portal after identity verification.

---

## Where can I view my invoices?

Invoices are available in the customer portal under **Billing > Invoices**.

---

## How do I check my current subscription?

Customers can view their active subscription through the mobile app or customer portal.

---

## How do I report a service issue?

Customers can create a support ticket using the mobile app, website, or customer support hotline.

---

# Escalation Criteria

Escalate if:

- The requested information is unavailable.
- The customer asks for account-specific changes.
- Identity verification is required before answering.
