# Billing Frequently Asked Questions

## Purpose
Provide standard responses for common billing inquiries.

## Scope
Applicable to all customers.

## Business Rules
- AI may explain invoices and billing dates.
- AI must not approve refunds or billing adjustments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is my bill generated?
Bills are generated on the customer's monthly billing cycle.

### How can I pay my bill?
Payments can be made through the mobile app, website, or authorized payment centers.

### Why is my bill higher this month?
Possible reasons include additional services, roaming charges, or overdue balances.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate:
- Refund requests
- Billing disputes
- Suspected incorrect charges
