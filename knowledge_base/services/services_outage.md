# Service Outage Handling Procedure

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Department:** Network Operations
**Owner:** NOC Team
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-20

---

# Purpose

Provide a consistent process for responding to customers affected by planned or unplanned service outages.

---

# Scope

Applies to broadband, mobile, and fixed-line services.

---

# Business Rules

- Always check the outage management system before troubleshooting.
- Do not ask customers to perform unnecessary troubleshooting during a confirmed outage.
- Never provide estimated restoration times unless officially published.

---

# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1. Check whether an outage exists in the customer's area.
2. If an outage is confirmed:
   - Inform the customer.
   - Share the official estimated restoration time (if available).
   - Apologize for the inconvenience.
3. If no outage exists:
   - Continue with standard troubleshooting procedures.

---

# Planned Maintenance

Customers should be informed of:

- Maintenance window
- Expected impact
- Estimated completion time

---

# Unplanned Outages

Inform customers that:

- Engineers are actively investigating.
- Service restoration updates will be communicated through official channels.

---

# Escalation Criteria

Escalate when:

- The customer reports a safety-related issue.
- The outage is not visible in monitoring systems but affects multiple customers.
- Critical business customers are impacted.
