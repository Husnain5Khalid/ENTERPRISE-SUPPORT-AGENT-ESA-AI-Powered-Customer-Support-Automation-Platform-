# Subscription Management Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Department:** Customer Operations  
**Owner:** Product Management Team  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-20

---

# Purpose

This policy defines the rules and procedures for upgrading, downgrading, and cancelling customer subscription plans.

---

# Scope

Applicable to all prepaid and postpaid mobile and internet customers.

---

# Business Rules

- Customers may upgrade their subscription at any time.
- Downgrades become effective from the next billing cycle.
- Plan cancellation requires identity verification.
- Active promotional offers may be lost after changing plans.
- AI can provide plan information but cannot force plan changes without customer confirmation.

---

# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Upgrade

1. Verify customer identity.
2. Display available plans.
3. Confirm customer's selection.
4. Process the upgrade.
5. Notify the customer of the activation time.

## Downgrade

1. Verify customer identity.
2. Explain the effective date.
3. Confirm customer approval.
4. Schedule the downgrade.

## Cancellation

1. Verify customer identity.
2. Inform the customer about remaining balances.
3. Confirm cancellation request.
4. Process cancellation.

---

# Exceptions

- Corporate accounts require manual approval.
- Contract plans cannot be cancelled before the contract period ends without applicable charges.

---

# Escalation Criteria

Escalate when:

- Corporate accounts are involved.
- Contract disputes occur.
- Cancellation penalties are questioned.
- Customer requests exceptions to company policy.
