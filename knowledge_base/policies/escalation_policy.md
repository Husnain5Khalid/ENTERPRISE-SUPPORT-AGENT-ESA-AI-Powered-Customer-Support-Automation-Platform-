# Ticket Escalation Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Department:** Customer Support  
**Owner:** Support Operations Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-20

---

# Purpose

Define situations where an AI agent must stop processing and transfer the ticket to a human support representative.

---

# Scope

Applies to all customer support tickets handled by the Enterprise Support Agent.

---

# Business Rules

The AI agent must never make decisions outside documented company policies.

---

# Mandatory Escalation Cases

- Refund requests
- Billing disputes
- Fraud reports
- Identity theft
- Legal complaints
- Regulatory requests
- Data privacy concerns
- VIP customer complaints
- Service compensation requests
- Low confidence responses (<80%)

---

# Escalation Procedure

1. Stop automated processing.
2. Record the reason for escalation.
3. Update ticket status to "Escalated".
4. Assign the ticket to the appropriate support queue.
5. Notify the customer that the request is being reviewed.

---

# Escalation Teams

| Issue | Team |
|------|------|
| Fraud | Fraud Investigation |
| Billing | Billing Operations |
| Legal | Legal Department |
| Technical | Network Operations |
| VIP | Premium Support |
